2 · Market research

Sources are listed on the last page. Labels: Verified = read on the vendor's official page; Claim / third-party = vendor claim or an aggregator, blog or competitor article (treat as indicative and possibly biased); Observation = my reading of the research.

How the market is layered

  • Enterprise chains: Zenoti (custom quotes, multi-location, payroll). Out of reach and out of scope for small salons.
  • India-fit suites for small and mid salons: Zylu, MioSalon, DINGG, Salonist. Booking, billing, GST, staff and commission, WhatsApp; roughly ₹800 to ₹4,000 a month with WhatsApp often extra.
  • Freemium marketplace: Fresha. Free core, paid add-ons, and a fee on new clients it brings.
  • Free or cheap expense/billing tools: Khatabook (free), Vyapar (about ₹2,400 to ₹3,500 a year). Not salon-specific.
  • Freelancers and agencies: websites and Google profile work, one-time or monthly.
  • Notebook, WhatsApp, Instagram DM, Excel: what most small salons actually use.

Prices are as reported in October 2026 and change often. MioSalon and DINGG do not publish prices on their official pages, so the figures shown are third-party reports. Fresha figures are largely US-dollar; confirm India pricing before quoting.

Which products are 'covered the market' and what is trending

  • Observation: the India-fit suites (Zylu, Mio, DINGG, Salonist) already cover booking, billing, GST, staff, commission and expense tracking for small and mid salons. Competing with them feature for feature is not a good use of a small team.
  • Verified: MioSalon documents an expense and cash-register module; DINGG lists expense tracking, attendance, salary and commission. Expense management is therefore a normal expectation, not a novelty. Our difference must be the owner-friendly, local-language, done-for-you experience, not the feature name.
  • Claim / third-party: the major global platforms and India-fit suites are moving toward WhatsApp automation, memberships and loyalty, AI-assisted support, and marketplace discovery.
  • Claim / third-party: salons without automated reminders report around 20% no-shows; reminders and deposits are reported to cut this substantially; about 64% of salon customers are reported to discover their current salon on Instagram. Treat as directional.
  • Verified (Google): local results rank on relevance, distance and prominence, and 'there's no way to request or pay for a better local ranking on Google'. So the Google Maps offer must be profile quality, reviews and website, never a ranking promise.

The gap we can credibly fill

Observation: apps are built for salons that already want software. Many small Gujarat salons are not there yet: they are not found on Google Maps, have no website, and run on WhatsApp and a diary. A local-language, Google-first, done-for-you ladder (get found, get booked, know your numbers) meets them where they are, then grows into software. That is the wedge.

What Gujarat and India buyers expect differently

  • WhatsApp is the booking channel; many owners will not install another app.
  • UPI and cash; GST billing is not always a daily need for small parlours.
  • Price sensitivity is high and one-time lumps feel heavy: show a monthly equivalent and a small first step.
  • Trust is personal: visits, referrals and a visible local reference matter more than slides.
  • Gujarati comfort in speech and WhatsApp, English for technical words (booking, Google Maps, dashboard).

Build approach

  • One reusable template, many salons. One codebase with per-salon configuration (name, colours, services, language, stylists) on a shared managed backend. Do not hand-build each client; that kills margin.
  • Keep cost per client small. Estimated infrastructure about ₹1,500 to ₹3,500 per client per year (assumption; use your real hosting bill).
  • Use what exists. Open-source or managed services for authentication, database and email; WhatsApp click-to-chat first, API later.
  • Language built in. Every public page and the dashboard labels support Gujarati and English from day one.

Out of scope for now

GST billing and POS, inventory, native iOS/Android apps and multi-branch reporting. Competitors are strong and these have high build and support cost. If a salon needs them, integrate with the tool it already uses.

Two-minute explanation

Most customers find a salon by searching on their phone. We make sure you appear with correct hours, services, photos and reviews, and a Gujarati and English website that explains your services and prices. Booking is one tap on WhatsApp. If you want more, your requests land in a simple dashboard with client notes, and later your daily expenses and salaries are kept in one place with a monthly summary. You choose the step; we only build what fits.

Discovery questions (use 2–3 per meeting)

  • How do most new clients find your salon today?
  • Do clients book by call, WhatsApp, Instagram, walk-in or website?
  • What happens when you search 'salon near me' on your phone?
  • How do you keep track of tomorrow's appointments?
  • What do you keep about returning clients, and where?
  • How do you track daily expenses, salaries and vendor dues?
  • How long does month-end take?
  • Which tools do you already use and like?
  • One location or several?
  • What is the one thing you most want to be simpler?
